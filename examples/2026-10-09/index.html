$1.19 billion in crypto positions closed as Ether traders take the worst hit

October 9, 2026 · 8:00 AM ET

Headline stories

Ether traders bear the worst of a $1.19 billion crypto selloff.

Late Thursday's decline closed $1.19 billion in borrowed crypto positions over 24 hours, more than $1 billion of them bets on higher prices. Ether traders lost about $356 million versus $298 million for Bitcoin, despite Ether's much smaller market value. The largest single closure was a nearly $20 million Ether position on Hyperliquid. Bitcoin dipped toward $80,400 before recovering above $82,000 early Friday.

Sources: coindesk.com

Thailand sets rules for domestic Bitcoin and Ether ETFs.

Thailand's securities regulator issued rules Friday for locally listed funds holding Bitcoin or Ether, with the rules scheduled to take effect October 16. Licensed custodians and a minimum 80% average exposure to the fund's chosen asset are required. Investors cannot borrow from brokers to buy the funds. This is a regulatory opening, not confirmation that any fund has begun trading.

Sources: theblock.co, coindesk.com

Culture and drama

Robinhood Chain's new $PAPER pool draws nearly $9 million in trading.

The pool opened early Friday and recorded $8.84 million in 24-hour trading, with a tracker-estimated fully diluted value of $13.87 million. Its displayed gain of more than 500,000% starts from a tiny opening price and does not represent a typical trader's return. Separately, Thursday's $SWARM pool traded $11.19 million and showed a 6,345% gain from its opening price. Neither ticker is evidence of a Robinhood-issued asset.

Sources: geckoterminal.com, geckoterminal.com

Fresh Solana meme pools jump even as Ether meme favorite CLAUS sinks.

A $TikTok pool opened Thursday evening and traded $6.56 million while rising about 1,093% from its opening price. Friday's $QI pool gained about 2,873% with $3.03 million traded; $PQC, opened Thursday morning, gained 1,264% with $6.05 million traded. On Ethereum, the top trending $CLAUS pool fell 74.9% on $6.50 million in trading. Pool opening prices can make percentage changes look spectacular.

Sources: geckoterminal.com, geckoterminal.com, geckoterminal.com

Builders and launches

Meanwhile raises $37.5 million to expand Bitcoin life insurance.

The Bermuda-based company announced the round Thursday; Bain Capital Crypto led it, alongside Haun Ventures, Framework Ventures, Pantera Capital and others. Meanwhile says it has signed 15 broker partners and is expanding Bitcoin-denominated life insurance outside the United States. Its total funding now exceeds $180 million. This is a funding announcement, not a new token launch.

Sources: theblock.co, businesswire.com
